🔭 Scouted · April 2026 · Gateway Classic Exploré · Avril 2026 · Classique d'Entrée

Carcassonne

Hans im Glück · Klaus-Jürgen Wrede · 2000

👥 2–5 Players ⏱ 30–45 min ⚖ Weight 1.9 / 5

Twenty-five years on, Carcassonne remains the cleanest tile-laying engine ever built. Each turn you draw one square and place it — adding a road, a city wall, a meadow — and decide whether to commit a meeple as a knight, monk, or farmer. Simple to teach in five minutes, but the farmer endgame rewards Antonin's silent territorial accumulation, while Audrey will quietly insert thieves into half-built roads to harvest someone else's work. Every family collection earns its first credit by owning this game. Vingt-cinq ans plus tard, Carcassonne reste le moteur de pose de tuiles le plus pur jamais conçu. Chaque tour, vous piochez une tuile et la placez — route, muraille, prairie — puis décidez de placer un meeple en chevalier, moine ou paysan. Cinq minutes d'apprentissage, mais le décompte final des paysans récompense l'accumulation silencieuse d'Antonin, et Audrey glissera son voleur dans la route à demi-finie d'un autre pour récolter son travail.

Hot Streak

CMYK Games · Jon Perry · 2025

👥 2–6 Players ⏱ 30–45 min ⚖ Weight 1.5 / 5

Imagine the racetrack at Royal Ascot — except the horses are a hot dog, an angler fish, a king, and a bear, and they all start running before anyone has placed a bet. CMYK's newest is a non-stop betting/racing screamfest with chunky mascot figures and an absurdly long pull-out track. Players draft betting tickets in two passes (first round forward, second round in reverse), choosing safe payouts or flipping for double-or-nothing. Then the deck rips through itself — mascots swerve lanes, knock each other over, occasionally sprint backwards. This one is for Audrey. She will love the risk-flip mechanic and the dopamine of a bluff payoff. Caveat for Antonin: outcomes are largely uncontrolled; he may resent the variance. Best deployed as a 30-minute warm-up before a longer game, or as the headline event with partners and friends present. Duel for Cardia

Hans im Glück · Mathieu Rivero & Faouzi Boughida · 2025

👥 2 Players ⏱ 15–20 min ⚖ Weight 1.8 / 5

Both players start with the same 16-card deck — numbered 1 to 16, four factions (Rebellion, Academy, Guild, Dynasty), each card carrying its own ability. Players reveal simultaneously; the higher number wins the encounter and claims a signet, but the loser's card triggers its ability. Sometimes losing pays better than winning — abilities can rewrite past encounters or steal your opponent's hand. Win five signets, hit a Djinn auto-win, or starve your opponent of cards. This is Audrey's partner's home turf: simultaneous-reveal duels with branching combos are exactly the puzzle space he excels in. Pair this with Toy Battle as the cabinet's two go-to 2-player duels — Toy Battle for raw territory, Cardia for combo discovery and bluff. Les deux joueurs partent avec le même deck de 16 cartes — numérotées 1 à 16, quatre factions (Rébellion, Académie, Guilde, Dynastie), chaque carte portant son pouvoir. Révélation simultanée ; le numéro supérieur remporte la rencontre et un sceau, mais la carte perdante déclenche son effet. Parfois perdre rapporte plus que gagner — les pouvoirs réécrivent les rencontres passées ou volent la main adverse. Terrain de prédilection du partenaire d'Audrey : les duels à révélation simultanée avec combinaisons branchantes sont exactement l'espace de devinettes où il excelle. Eternal Decks

Hiroaki Yamamoto (Hiroken) · 2025

A cooperative deckbuilder with a difference — players begin with a tiny five-card deck and acquire cards as they progress through six escalating stages: collect points, survive a ghost town, navigate a labyrinth, defeat a boss. Cards must be placed into three rows under strict colour and numbering constraints, demanding spatial logic and tight communication limits between players. The black-on-purple woodcut art is the most distinctive visual direction of any 2025 release — folk-illustration density, almost broadside-poster in feel. Audrey's partner will love this game: the constraint puzzle is exactly his idiom. Antonin will engage as long as he is allowed to lead the deck-acquisition strategy. A natural complement to Sleeping Gods on the cooperative shelf — shorter, more puzzle-driven, less narrative. Un deckbuilder coopératif particulier — les joueurs débutent avec un mini-deck de cinq cartes et acquièrent de nouvelles cartes à travers six étapes croissantes : collecter des points, survivre dans une ville fantôme, traverser un labyrinthe, vaincre un boss. Les cartes doivent être placées sur trois rangées sous contraintes strictes de couleur et de numérotation, exigeant logique spatiale et communication limitée. Cyclades

Matagot · Bruno Cathala & Ludovic Maublanc · 2009 (Legendary Edition 2023)

Bruno Cathala — half of the Bruno-Bruno duo behind Mr. Jack and Five Tribes — and Ludovic Maublanc deliver one of the cleanest auction-driven war games ever made. Each round, players bid on the favour of five Greek gods: Zeus (priests and temples), Ares (armies), Poseidon (fleets), Athena (wisdom and philosophers), Apollo (gold). Whoever wins the god takes their action; everyone else must redirect. Build two metropolises and you win — but they can be taken from you by force. This is Antonin's perfect game. The auction is pure economic optimisation, gold management is constant, and the path to victory rewards stockpiling. Audrey will read the table, hold back, and steal a metropolis on the final turn. Maud will quietly assemble a philosopher-and-temple combo while no one is looking. The 2023 Legendary Edition consolidates all expansions and ships with vastly upgraded miniatures — this is the version to buy. Chaque tour, les joueurs enchérissent sur les faveurs des cinq dieux grecs : Zeus (prêtres et temples), Arès (armées), Poséidon (flottes), Athéna (philosophes), Apollon (or). 🔭 Scouted · April 2026 · ✦ Artisan Production Exploré · Avril 2026 · ✦ Production Artisanale

Parks

Keymaster Games · Henry Audubon · 2019

👥 1–5 Players ⏱ 30–60 min ⚖ Weight 2.0 / 5

A walk through the U.S. National Parks system, designed in collaboration with the Fifty-Nine Parks Print Series — every park card carries an actual silkscreen poster from the project. Two hikers walk a seasonal trail that resets every round; each space lets you collect resources (sun, water, mountain, forest, wildlife) which you then spend to visit named parks.
