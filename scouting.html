The Ludo Assessment Framework Le Cadre d'Évaluation Ludo

Every game Ludo scouts is assessed on the same six dimensions — so you can compare any new discovery against your existing collection in a single glance. Chaque jeu exploré par Ludo est évalué selon les mêmes six dimensions — pour comparer toute découverte avec votre collection existante en un coup d'œil.

01
Family Fit Adéquation Familiale

Where does this game sit in the family's existing repertoire? Who will love it (Antonin, Audrey, mixed group, new players)? Which existing games does it complement or replace? Où se situe ce jeu dans le répertoire existant? Qui va l'adorer? Quels jeux existants complète-t-il ou remplace-t-il?

02
Time to Learn Temps d'Apprentissage

How long before you can actually start playing? 5 minutes (Skull), 30 minutes (Catan), 2 hours (John Company)? Rated from ★ (instant) to ★★★★★ (full evening of rules). Combien de temps avant de pouvoir vraiment jouer? De ★ (immédiat) à ★★★★★ (soirée entière de règles).

03
Game Duration Durée de Partie

Realistic play time for this family. Quick opener (15–30 min)? Main event (90–150 min)? Marathon session (4+ hours, Diplomacy territory)? Temps de jeu réaliste pour cette famille. Ouvreur rapide (15–30 min)? Événement principal (90–150 min)? Session marathon (4h+)?

04
Player Count Nombre de Joueurs

Does it work for 3–4 (core family) or 5–6 (with partners and guests)? Does player count change the dynamic significantly? Is there a sweet spot? Fonctionne-t-il pour 3–4 (famille de base) ou 5–6 (avec partenaires)? Le nombre de joueurs change-t-il significativement la dynamique?

05
Game Dynamic Dynamique de Jeu

Is it adversarial, collaborative, or mixed? Does it favour Antonin's resource instincts, Audrey's deception, or Audrey's partner's code-cracking? How much luck vs. skill? Adversarial, collaboratif ou mixte? Favorise-t-il les instincts de ressources d'Antonin, la tromperie d'Audrey? Quelle proportion chance/compétence?

06
Aesthetic Assessment Évaluation Esthétique

Production quality, materials, historical grounding, special editions. Is this an object worth owning for life? Are there artisan upgrades (Etsy, Kickstarter, antique dealers)? Oath: Chronicles of Empire & Exile

Leder Games · Cole Wehrle · Kyle Ferrin · 2021

👥 1–6 Players ⏱ 2–3 hours ⚖ BGG 4.3

Cole Wehrle and Kyle Ferrin's most ambitious work. Each session of Oath reshapes the game's card pool, accumulating the wreckage of previous civilisations like an archaeological record. A game that rewards the same group playing it across many months — exactly the kind of long-arc investment this family might enjoy. Kyle Ferrin's illustration style pushes into expressionist abstraction — among the most striking visual languages in modern board gaming. Le travail le plus ambitieux de Cole Wehrle. Chaque session d'Oath remodèle le pool de cartes, accumulant les décombres des civilisations précédentes. Un jeu qui récompense le même groupe jouant pendant de nombreux mois — exactement le type d'investissement sur le long terme que cette famille pourrait apprécier.

Root

Leder Games · Cole Wehrle · Kyle Ferrin · 2018

👥 2–4 Players ⏱ 60–90 min ⚖ BGG 3.7

A ferocious asymmetric political game dressed as a children's woodland story. Four factions — Marquise de Cat, Eyrie Dynasties, Woodland Alliance, Vagabond — each play by completely different rules. The deliberate contrast between Kyle Ferrin's whimsical storybook art and the cutthroat political mechanics makes this one of the most cleverly designed games of the past decade. Audrey will love it. Un jeu politique asymétrique féroce habillé en histoire de forêt pour enfants. Quatre factions, chacune jouant selon des règles complètement différentes. Audrey adorera.

Sleeping Gods

Red Raven Games · Ryan Laukat · 2021

👥 1–4 Players ⏱ 60–120 min/session ⚖ BGG 2.8

A cooperative campaign where players navigate a 1929 steamship through a mythic hand-drawn ocean. Ryan Laukat designed, illustrated, and wrote the entire game himself — a singular artistic achievement. The illustrated atlas (a foldout map of uncommon beauty) and the storybook narrative system make this one of the most beautiful game objects produced in the last decade. Exceptional for couples or a smaller family evening. Une campagne coopérative naviguant un vapeur de 1929 à travers un océan mythique dessiné à la main. Ryan Laukat a tout conçu, illustré et écrit seul. L'atlas illustré fait de ce jeu l'un des objets les plus beaux de la dernière décennie.

The Estates (Il Vecchio)

Capstone Games · Toryo Hatsumi · 2018

👥 2–5 Players ⏱ 40–60 min ⚖ BGG 2.8

A brutal real-estate bidding game where incomplete buildings score negatively. Players bid on components (buildings, mayors, extensions) and must decide whether to invest in rows they control — or sabotage opponents by forcing them to buy components that hurt them. Nastier than it looks. Antonin's resource management instincts will immediately recognise the opportunity for economic domination. Perfect family game duration (under 1 hour). Un jeu d'enchères immobilières brutal où les bâtiments incomplets marquent négativement. Antonin adorera dominer économiquement. Durée parfaite pour la famille (moins d'1 heure).

🎓 Learning: ★★☆☆☆ Fast setup ⚔ Dynamic: Adversarial · Bidding · Nasty 🎨 Aesthetic: ★★★☆☆ Clean, geometric 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Fit: Excellent · 3–5 players · Antonin's game

Mughal Indian Ivory Chess Sets

19th Century · India · Auction Houses

Hand-carved ivory or bone chess sets from the Mughal period (18th–19th century) represent the pinnacle of game artisanship. Pieces feature ornate turbaned kings, elephant-mounted rooks, and cavalry knights carved with extraordinary detail. Perfect alignment with Chris's historical aesthetic: Mughal India, colonial period, noble materials. Source: Bonhams London, Christie's South Kensington, Sotheby's, and specialist Indian antique dealers in London and Paris.
